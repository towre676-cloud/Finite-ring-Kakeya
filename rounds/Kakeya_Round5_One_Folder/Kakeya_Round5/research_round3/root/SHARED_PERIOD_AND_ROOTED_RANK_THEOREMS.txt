SHARED PERIODS AND ROOTED LOW-RANK CAPACITY

Scope: both Z/4Z and F2[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), in arbitrary binary dimension.
These are analytic compatibility theorems. Profile exclusions using them
must separately quantify over every permitted shape mode and support.

1. EXACT COVERAGE OBJECT

Let V=F2^n and let A_a be the tangent fibre at residue a. For d!=0 write
P_a(d)={x+<d>: x and x+d belong to A_a}, a subset of Q_d=V/<d>.
Its size is p_a(d), the number of unordered d-pairs in A_a. The actual
capacity of an edge {a,b}, d=a+b, is

    C_ab=|P_a(d)+P_b(d)+c_R(a,d)|.

The carry c_R is a quotient translation. All bounds below hold uniformly
over internal translations and both ring carries. Empty pair sets give
capacity zero.

2. SHARED OWN-PERIOD THEOREM

Translate a distinguished hub residue to zero. Let a,b be distinct nonzero
residues. Suppose A_a+a=A_a and A_b+b=A_b. Then, on d=a+b,

    p_a(d) and p_b(d) are even,
    C_ab <= p_a(d)*p_b(d)/2.                              (P)

Proof. The class h=a+<d>=b+<d> is nonzero: otherwise a is0 or d,
which would make a or b zero. Translation by a preserves every d-pair in
A_a, so P_a(d)+h=P_a(d). Similarly P_b(d)+h=P_b(d). Each pair set is
a union of two-element h-orbits, proving evenness. The involution
(u,v)->(u+h,v+h) on P_a(d) x P_b(d) is fixed-point-free and leaves u+v
unchanged. Every output therefore has at least two representations, and
(P) follows. The empty cases are immediate.

More generally, if P,Q share a translation-period subgroup H, then
|P+Q|<=|P||Q|/|H|, by the same diagonal H-action. The own-period theorem
forces such an H without knowing the detailed shapes.

3. TWO OWN-POSITIVE LOW-RANK FIBRES

For each selected nonzero residue a, suppose the affine hull of A_a has
direction space H_a of dimension at most k, and p_a(a)>0. Thus a lies in H_a.
For an active edge to b, d=a+b lies in H_a and H_b. Hence

    span(a,b) subset H_a intersect H_b.

Because a,b are distinct nonzero binary vectors, this span has dimension2.
The pair-label set P_a(d) lies in an affine subspace of Q_d with direction
H_a/<d>. Consequently

    C_ab <= 2^(dim H_a + dim H_b - dim(H_a intersect H_b)-1)
         <= 2^(2k-3).                                   (R1)

In particular k=3 gives an actual edge cap8 for any two own-positive
fibres of affine rank at most3, including appropriate six-, seven-, and
eight-point fibres. It is not a bound on the coarser scalar M(p,q).

4. OUTSIDE-SUPPORT SUBGRAPH THEOREM

Let S contain0 and r distinct nonzero selected residues. At every selected
residue a, assume the fibre has affine rank at most k and p_a(a)>0, with
k>=2. Consider only selected-selected edges {a,b} whose direction a+b
lies outside S. Then

    sum C_ab <= 2^(2k-4)*floor(r^2/2).                    (R2)

For k=3 this is4*floor(r^2/2), giving72 for six selected fibres.
For k=2 it is floor(r^2/2). No affine-flat assumption is necessary: only
the dimension bound on each fibre's affine hull is used.

Proof. Put L=2^(2k-4). Equation(R1) bounds every edge by2L. Consider a
triangle of positive outside-support edges, with residues a,b,c. The three
residues are linearly independent: the only possible dependence among
three distinct nonzero binary vectors would be a+b=c, but then the edge
direction a+b belongs to S. At vertex a, own positivity and the active
edges put a,b,c in H_a; the same holds at the other two vertices. Thus
span(a,b,c) lies in all three direction spaces and has dimension3.

If k=2, such a triangle is impossible. If k>=3, every edge in it has
actual capacity at most2^(2k-4)=L. Therefore an edge of capacity greater
than L has no common neighbour joined positively to both endpoints.

Normalize edge weights by L. All weights are in[0,2]. Every edge of weight
greater than1 belongs to no positive triangle. The total weight is at most
floor(r^2/2), by induction. If all edges have weight at most1, use
binom(r,2)<=floor(r^2/2). Otherwise remove an edge uv of weight>1 and its
endpoints. Every other vertex meets at most one of u,v positively, so the
removed weight is at most2+2(r-2)=2r-2. Induction on r-2 gives

    2r-2+floor((r-2)^2/2)=floor(r^2/2).

Multiplying by L proves(R2). This proof permits arbitrary real edge
weights in[0,2]; it does not assume every actual sumset is a power of two.

5. SAFE COMBINATION WITH OTHER HEAVY-EDGE DEFICITS

Suppose a heavy-edge baseline B has certified endpoint losses l_v, and a
selected low-rank subgroup U has baseline B_U but actual subgroup capacity
at most C_U. Let D_U=max(0,B_U-C_U). Endpoint losses at vertices outside U
charge only edges not internal to U. Their combined loss is at least

    D_out=max(max_{v not in U} l_v, ceil(sum_{v not in U}l_v/2)).

The internal loss D_U and this external loss concern disjoint edge sets.
Thus a valid total loss lower bound is

    max(max_v l_v, ceil(sum_v l_v/2), D_U+D_out).

It would generally be invalid to add D_U to the total endpoint-loss bound,
because the latter may already charge the same internal edges.

6. FINITE CONTROLS AND SCOPE

verify_shared_geometry.py checks the shared-period involution exhaustively
on normalized independent residues in F2^4, checks the low-rank flat edge
and triangle geometry directly in F2^5, and exhausts weighted graphs through
five vertices. These controls supplement the analytic proofs; they do not
replace their arbitrary-dimensional arguments.

The prior round's unrooted cube theorem remains valid. The stronger bound
here uses the own-positive hypothesis and restriction to directions outside
the support; omitting either hypothesis would invalidate this stronger
argument. All exclusion counts are recorded separately in branch ledgers.

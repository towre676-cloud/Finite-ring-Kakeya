TWO MINIMUM IMAGES SUFFICE WHEN ONE IS AN ADDITIVE COSET
27 October 2026. Conditional all-dimension theorem.

Setup. R is Z/4Z or F2[e]/(e^2), G=R^n, pi is 2 or e, N=2^n,
and S_t=(f+t Id)(G), K_f=union_t S_t for any f:G->G.

THEOREM. Suppose two unit-separated images S_s,S_t each have N
points, and at least one of those two images is an additive coset.
Then |K_f|>=3N-2. The other two images need not have size N or
be additive cosets, and f need not be additive.

Preliminary subgroup-transversal lemma.
Let A,C be subgroups of a finite abelian group G of order N^2,
with |A|=N and |C|=2N, and let B be an N-point transversal for A
containing 0. Put h=|A intersect C|. Then

    |A union B union C| >= 3N-2.

Indeed, C meets exactly |C|/h=2N/h cosets of A. Since B has at
most one point in each, |B intersect C|<=min(N,2N/h). Also
A intersect B={0}, and this point lies in C. Therefore

  |A union B union C|
    =4N-h-|B intersect C|
    >=4N-h-min(N,2N/h).

For h=1, the final expression is at least 3N-1. For 2<=h<=N,
h+2N/h<=N+2, equivalently (h-2)(h-N)<=0. This proves the lemma.

Main proof.
An affine permutation of the parameter t, accompanied by a unit
rescaling of the argument v, sends the specified unit-separated
pair to parameters 0,1 and preserves the union of all four images.
Translate by -f(0). We may therefore write A=S_0 and B=S_1,
with |A|=|B|=N, A an additive subgroup, and 0 in both sets.

The pair map v -> (f(v),f(v)+v) is a bijection onto A x B.
Consequently the difference map (a,b)->b-a is bijective, B is a
transversal for A, and

    C=S_pi=(1-pi)A+pi B.

The subgroup U=(1-pi)A has order N, and C is a union of U-cosets.
Its cardinality is therefore a positive integer multiple of N.

If |C|>=3N, the theorem is immediate. If |C|=N, there are three
N-point images, so THREE_MINIMUM_IMAGES_THEOREM.txt applies.
The remaining case is |C|=2N. Because 0 is in B, one of C's
two U-cosets is U. Its other coset is U+h0 for some h0 in pi B.
Every element of pi G has additive order at most two, so 2h0=0.
It follows that U union (U+h0) is itself an additive subgroup.
The preliminary lemma now applies to A,B,C and proves
|K_f|>=|A union B union C|>=3N-2. QED.

Scope. The remaining two-minimum-images case is a genuine exact
factorization A x B -> G in which both A and B are noncosets.
Nothing here reduces all factorizations to the coset case. Nor is
it proved that an arbitrary smaller Kakeya set admits two minimum
parameter images. This is an enlarged conditional theorem only.

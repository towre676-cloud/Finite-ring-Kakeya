SHARED THREE-FLAT FIBRES: A GLOBAL EDGE-CAPACITY THEOREM

This is a separate analytic compatibility theorem. No exclusion count in the
round2 ledger is credited to it. It is a candidate constraint for the next
stage using actual tangent subspaces and residue adjacency.

Let r residue fibres in a Kakeya problem over either target ring be affine
three-dimensional binary flats (each of size8), with tangent direction
spaces H_i. Consider only edges between these r residues. Define each edge's
capacity as the actual quotient sumset cardinality
|P_i(d)+P_j(d)+carry|, with d=a_i+a_j and P_i(d) the set of tangent pair labels
in V/<d>. This is not the coarser scalar cap M(p_i(d),p_j(d)). The sum of these
actual capacities is at most

    8*floor(r^2/2).                                           (1)

In particular, six such fibres have mutual capacity at most144, whereas
independent edge caps would permit16*binom(6,2)=240.

Proof. An edge i--j has residue direction d=a_i+a_j. It is active only if
d belongs to H_i intersect H_j. Its pair-label sets in V/<d> are affine
two-flats. If k=dim(H_i intersect H_j), their sum has dimension5-k, so
its exact cardinality is2^(5-k), one of4,8,16. A ring carry translates this
sumset and leaves its cardinality unchanged.

If i,j,k form a triangle of active edges, their three nonzero residue
differences span a two-space U. At each vertex, the two incident differences
belong to its tangent three-space. Therefore all three tangent spaces contain
U. Every edge of that triangle has intersection dimension at least2 and
capacity at most8. Consequently an edge of capacity16 lies in no triangle
of positive-capacity edges.

Divide all edge capacities by8. The resulting weighted graph has positive
weights1/2,1,2, and an edge of weight2 has no common positive neighbor.
We prove its total weight is at mostfloor(r^2/2) by induction. If there is
no weight2 edge, total weight is at mostbinom(r,2), which is at mostthe
claimed bound. Otherwise remove a weight2 edgeuv and its two endpoints.
Every remaining vertex is adjacent positively to at mostone ofu,v, so
removed weight is at most

    2+2(r-2)=2r-2.

Induction on the remaining r-2 vertices gives

    total <=2r-2+floor((r-2)^2/2)=floor(r^2/2).

Multiplying by8 proves (1). The casesr0,r1 are immediate. The argument
holds in every ambient binary dimension in which these flats exist; in
smaller dimensions some of the permitted edge weights are simply absent.

Independent review: root checked the quotient sumset dimension, the common
triangle two-space, the induction step, and carry invariance. This theorem
uses actual common fibre geometry, so a weight8 fibre may be treated this
way only after it is known to be an affine three-flat. It must not be
applied to all eight-point fibres merely from their cardinality.

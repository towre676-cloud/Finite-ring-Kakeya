OUTSIDE-SUPPORT PAIR-BUDGET THEOREM

Scope: both R=Z/4Z and R=F2[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), all dimensions.
Let V=F2^n, N=2^n, and let A_a be the tangent fibre at residue a.
Let S={a: |A_a|>=2} be the pair-active residue support. Translate a selected
hub residue to zero, so 0 belongs to S. For a in S\{0}, write w=|A_a|
and t=p_a(a), the number of own-direction unordered pairs in A_a.

Theorem. The pair counts which can contribute through the vertex a to
lifted directions whose residue d lies outside S satisfy

  sum_{d not in S, a+d in S} p_a(d) <= binom(w,2)-t(w-t).           (1)

Consequently, if t>=1 then this sum is at most binom(w-1,2).
If t=0 the bound is binom(w,2).

Proof. Nonzero directions other than a split into unordered blocks
{d,d+a}. If d is outside S and a+d is inside S, the mate direction d+a
cannot contribute to another outside-support edge at a: its opposite
endpoint is a+(d+a)=d, which is outside S and has no tangent pair.
Thus the sum on the left uses at most one entry from every block.

Partition A_a by cosets of <a>. There are t full cosets (two points
each) and s=w-2t singleton cosets. Consider an unordered pair of distinct
cosets. Its tangent differences lie in one block {d,d+a}:

  full/full: the two directional pair contributions are (2,2);
  full/single: they are (1,1);
  single/single: they are either (1,0) or (0,1).

For each block, the maximum of its two total contributions is at most
the sum of the maxima contributed by its coset pairs. Summing gives

  sum_blocks max(p_a(d),p_a(d+a))
    <= 2*binom(t,2)+t*s+binom(s,2)
     = binom(w,2)-t(w-t).

This proves (1). For 1<=t<=floor(w/2), t(w-t)>=w-1, giving the final
assertion. Carries in Z4 affect lift labels but not these pair counts.

WHY THE SUPPORT MAY IGNORE SINGLETON FIBRES

A fibre of size zero or one has no tangent pair in any direction, so it
cannot be an endpoint of a full primitive line. An outside-support
residue may still contain one point; the proof uses only the absence
of pairs. This is exactly the pair-active support used in our M19 lists.

APPLICATION TO THE MAXIMUM-19 PROFILE LISTS

For n=5 and support size k=11 or12, every d outside S must receive all16
projective lifts from nonhub edges. Therefore the sum of their capped
edge capacities is at least16*(32-k), respectively336 or320.

Use c_i=floor(w_i/2), and the new budget
 B_i=binom(w_i-1,2) for own-positive fibres,
 B_i=binom(w_i,2) for own-zero fibres.
The actual selected pair counts obey these budgets and the previously
proved maximum-direction quota r(w_i). Edges not serving outside-S
directions have zero effective capacity in this argument.

The existing heavy/light resource inequality remains valid on this
subgraph, enlarged to the complete graph for its upper bound. A light
endpoint pays ell*p to cover light/light contributions using
2*M(p,q)<=ell*(p+q); its remaining heavy-edge gains are optimized subject
to sum p<=B_i and at most r(w_i) counts equal to c_i. Heavy endpoints
similarly have incident deficits from their shared B_i budget. If the
sum of deficits is D and their maximum is Dmax, total heavy-edge loss
is at least max(Dmax,ceil(D/2)). This is subtracted from the complete
heavy-edge baseline. All arithmetic is integral and exact.

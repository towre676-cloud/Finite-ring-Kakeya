## What is now enforced

Each local directional allocation embeds in a complete realizable pair spectrum of one fibre. In the outside-support analysis it also embeds in a complete distinguished-direction paired-block spectrum. Own pair counts are linked to the hub contribution in the hub-inclusive screen. The last layer constrains the distribution of outside-support degrees and forces a sole hub edge to cover16 whenever a vertex has maximum outside degree.

All these tests still maximize different local endpoints separately. The actual fibre shapes, residue support adjacency, direction labels and quotient pair labels are not yet chosen together.

## Concrete remaining constraints

1. **Actual support adjacency.** The outside-support degree pattern does not say which pairs of fibre types are adjacent. There are only44 hub-marked difference-covering support cases at size11 and96 at size12 in the inherited affine census. A stronger search should fix one support, place the weight/flag types, and impose its actual edges. Symmetry of the marked support can reduce repeated placements. The new degree test produced only25 additional whole-profile exclusions, so another degree-only scan is not the proposed continuation.

2. **Own-direction coverage shared with offhub edges.** At residue a, the number of offhub residue edges in its own direction is (s-2-d_out(a))/2. If this number is0, the hub edge alone must cover all16 lifts. If it is1, the specific other edge has a forced residual demand after accounting for the hub. These demands couple the weights and pair counts at different vertices. The degree screen used only the weakest impossibility for own-zero/weight<=3 fibres at zero-offhub-edge directions.

3. **Direction labels within one fibre.** A pair spectrum is a multiset. The extremal pair directions of an actual fibre form its stabilizer or the odd-size near-period subspace; pair counts in sums of directions must respect these subspaces. The present assignment bounds freely permute counts and therefore relax this labelled geometry.

4. **One simultaneous shape choice.** The heavy/light local maxima may come from different affine fibre types in different roles. For example, an8-point affine3-flat has seven period directions, whereas a nonflat8-point fibre has at most one. A fixed fibre cannot use whichever of those alternatives is advantageous at each separate endpoint inequality.

5. **Exact quotient coverage.** Pair-count capacity is only an upper bound on P_a(d)+P_b(d)+carry. Actual16-point quotient coverage imposes common difference restrictions, Fourier cancellation and ring-specific carry shifts. Both rings share the current exclusions because translations do not change cardinality. A final realizability search must retain the carry for Z/4Z and zero carry for the dual-number ring.

## A practical order for the next proof search

Then impose period-subspace direction constraints and exact quotient sumset coverage only on the surviving placements. Every exclusion should record a checkable support/placement certificate. A whole weight/flag profile is closed only after every admissible marked support and placement is excluded.

1. THE PRECISE FINITE-RING SETUP

Put V=F2^n and N=2^n. Write ring points as a+pi*b, where pi=2 or epsilon.
The tangent fibre at residue a is A_a={b:a+pi*b belongs to K}. For d nonzero,
let p_a(d) count unordered pairs {b,b+d} in A_a. Then

  p_a(d)<=floor(|A_a|/2),
  sum_{d!=0}p_a(d)=binom(|A_a|,2).

A primitive line uses one tangent d-pair in each endpoint of the residue
edge {a,a+d}. Its projective lift label lies in V/<d>. In dimension5 there
are16 lift labels over each of31 nonzero residue directions. Consequently

  sum_{residue edges of direction d} p_a(d)*p_(a+d)(d)>=16.

The sharper edge cap M(p,q) is min(16,pq), except M(3,5)=13, M(3,6)=14,
M(3,7)=15 and their symmetric counterparts. These follow from the elementary
three-point sumset inequality |X+Y|<=min(3|Y|,|Y|+8,16) in F2^4.

The pair-active support is S={a:|A_a|>=2}. Singleton fibres contain no tangent
pair and cannot participate in any primitive line. They may be deleted for
lower-bound purposes. Every set under consideration must have S+S=V.

Both rings have the same necessary cardinality bounds. The Z4 carry changes
lift labels by a translation; the present inequalities retain valid upper
bounds for every such translation.

3. THE RESIDUE GEOMETRY THAT FINISHES THE SUPPORT PROOFS

Eight points T in F2^5 determine at most25 nonzero differences. Their number
of missing directions is either6 or at least9. If exactly6 are missing,
after translating T there is a basis h,b,c,d,e such that

  T=<h,b> union {c,d,e,h+c+d+e},
  F={b,b+h}+{c+d,c+e,d+e}.

Thus F is an affine3-flat with an affine line removed. It lies in a character
halfspace, and each residue has at most3 neighbours in T along directions F.
Nine points determine at most28 nonzero differences.

The eight-point plane-existence proof uses the augmented matrix with columns
(1,t). Its kernel has dimension at least2. With no weight-four relation,
two independent codewords and their sum would each have weight at least6,
total at least18. Each coordinate occurs zero or twice across these three
words, giving total at most16. The contradiction supplies an affine plane.
Its three repeated directions already reduce28 pairs to at most25 directions.

At equality the plane consumes all repeated differences. The four remaining
points lie in distinct nonzero cosets of its two-space, with sumfree quotient
labels. This forces the displayed normal form. The gap from6 to at least9
missing directions follows from the fact that three parallel pairs already
have duplicate excess8; otherwise every pair count is at most2 and duplicate
excess is three times the number of affine planes.

The nine-point bound also has an analytic augmented-code proof. It needs no
affine-orbit census. The complete arguments and direct normalized-set controls
are included in the package.

A memorable final middle-case contradiction is the profile

  (12,8^6,6,5,4,2^9).

Choose the first eight heavy fibres. At least six directions have no heavy-
heavy edge. Four low pairs are needed per missing direction, except that the
unique cap-six hub can save one pair in at most two directions. The low
fibres have total pair mass25, so the missing count must be exactly6.
Its normal form puts all missing directions in one halfspace. The five-point,
four-point, and nine two-point low fibres supply at most6+4+9=19 pairs across

5. AN ALL-DIMENSION OUTSIDE-SUPPORT PAIR BUDGET

Translate a selected hub residue to0. At a nonzero active residue a, let
w=|A_a| and t=p_a(a). For directions outside S that can meet an active
partner at a+d, we proved

  sum_{d not in S, a+d in S}p_a(d)<=binom(w,2)-t*(w-t).

If t>=1, the right side is at most binom(w-1,2). This weakly improves the
earlier budget binom(w,2)-1, strictly when w>=3 and the own direction is positive.

The reason is geometric: directions d and d+a form a block. If d is outside
S and a+d is inside S, the mate direction cannot also be used for an outside-
support edge. At most one direction from each block is selected.

Partition A_a into t full cosets of<a> and s=w-2t singleton cosets. A pair
of full cosets contributes(2,2) across one direction block; a full/single
pair contributes(1,1); two singleton cosets contribute(1,0) or(0,1).
Selecting one entry per block gives at most

  2*binom(t,2)+t*s+binom(s,2)=binom(w,2)-t*(w-t).

The argument is analytic, dimension independent, and valid for pair-active
support even when other residue fibres are singletons.

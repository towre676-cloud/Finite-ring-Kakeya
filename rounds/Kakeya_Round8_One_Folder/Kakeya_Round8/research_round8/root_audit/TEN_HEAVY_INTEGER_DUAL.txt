A short exact dual certificate for the ten-heavy case
====================================================

The geometry is a separate input.  The ten heavy residues split into
three U vertices and seven W vertices.  There are twenty-four directions
with exactly one heavy-heavy edge.  Each U vertex lies on nine of those
edges, and each W vertex on three.  U fibres have affine rank five; W
fibres have rank at least three, with at most two of rank three.  There
are three light fibres of weight two or three.  All fibres have weight
at most eleven, and the total weight is at most ninety-three.

Define pair prices

    f(0),...,f(5) = 0,1,4,9,16,17.

For quotient pair-sets of sizes p,q<=5, their sumset size B obeys

    2 B <= f(p)+f(q).

Product and ambient bounds prove this in every case except the sharper
3-by-5 case.  A three-set lies in an affine binary plane.  In each of
the four cosets of that plane, r points of the other set give at most
r+2 sums when the coset is occupied.  Thus B(5,3)<=5+8=13, proving
the remaining equality 2*13=9+17.  This uses no sumset census.

Let R_U be the sum of the nine largest f-values of a U-fibre's pair
spectrum and R_W the sum of the three largest for a W-fibre.  Let
R_L=10*choose(w,2) for a light fibre.  Charging the unique heavy-heavy
edges and then all edges touching light fibres gives the necessary bound

    768 = 2*16*24 <= sum_U R_U + sum_W R_W + sum_L R_L.

The light charge is valid because a light directional count is at most
one, its partner has at most five pairs, and each fibre-direction pair
belongs to at most one residue edge.  Charging an edge with two light
endpoints to either endpoint only weakens this estimate.

The complete inherited fibre census gives these small resource tables:

    weight             4   5   6   7   8   9   10  11
    U rank5, top9      -   -   9  18  35  65  114 121
    W rank3, top3      3  12  17  27  48   -    -   -
    W rank>=4, top3    -   3  12  17  27  48   49  51

A dash means no such fibre exists.  Every row satisfies, with integers
only,

    4 R_U <= 45 w + 6,
    4 R_W <= 45 w - 213 + 45*[rank(W)=3],
    4 R_L <= 45 w - 15.

Summing three U rows, seven W rows, and three light rows, and using the
at-most-two rank-three W fibres, yields

    4 (sum R) <= 45*93 + 3*6 - 7*213 + 2*45 - 3*15
               = 2757 < 3072 = 4*768.

Thus the resource is at most floor(2757/4)=689 although it must be at
least768.  The strict gap is315 after multiplication by four.  This
excludes the stated ten-heavy configuration.  No distinguished fibre
needs to have weight exactly eleven; the bound eleven is enough.

The rational coefficients were found by a small linear optimization.
Their verification requires no numerical optimizer: the accompanying
script reads the pinned census directly, checks all integer inequalities,
and independently recomputes the sharper budget dynamic-program values
663 (a weight-eleven hub in W) and685 (a weight-eleven hub in U).

Scope: the pair-price argument is elementary.  The resource table depends
on the inherited exhaustive fibre census, and application to every
ten-heavy support depends on the separately checked ten-set geometry.
The LP dual and its replay alone do not certify a whole support orbit.

ROUND 8: EXPLICIT HEAVY AND TETRAHEDRAL CAPACITY EXTENSIONS

Scope
-----
This is a one-way mathematical and code argument for the common necessary
relaxation in encode_strengthened.py. It concerns dimension five over both
Z/4Z and F2[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), a declared support of thirteen pair-active
residues, and fibre weights in the declared maximum interval. The baseline
remains the frozen Round6 joint-maxima encoder. Its census and one-way
soundness obligations are inherited. This text is not a proof-assistant
formalization.

Start from an actual Kakeya configuration satisfying the support, maximum
and total-weight hypotheses. The baseline soundness argument supplies its
actual census spectrum selectors z[v,i] and pair-count selectors y[v,d,p].
The extension reads these two explicit maps only. The implementation uses
the frozen audited encode_maximal_cosets.py with strengthen=False, which
also exposes the actual H/K selector maps for separately reviewed future
cuts. In that mode it emits the same baseline clauses and variable order;
the controls independently compare it to encode_joint_maxima.py. No
baseline auxiliary number, allocation order or clause position is inferred.

Heavy-fibre cardinality
----------------------
For each vertex v introduce h_v equivalent to the OR of the spectrum
selectors having weight at least four. Its defining CNF is

    (not h_v OR all eligible z) AND (not z OR h_v) for every eligible z.

Thus h_v=[actual fibre weight >=4]. An empty eligible domain forces h_v
false. With13 active fibres and all weights<=18, the preceding geometric
theorem requires at least8 heavy fibres. If all weights<=13, it requires
at least9. The theorem threshold is selected from the largest weight in
the actual selector domains, not from an unchecked caller assertion.

These are the written results in Round7 geometry/SUPPORT13_HEAVY_FIBRES.md
and SUPPORT13_EIGHT_HEAVY_COROLLARIES.md. The latter uses |D(H)|<=25 for
eight binary5 points. Five light fibres then supply at most15 directional
pairs, each charged at most6, whereas at least6 missing heavy directions
need96 units: 90<96. Neither of these two cardinality conclusions requires
the total-weight93 assumption. The baseline's bidirectional unary counter
encodes their indicated cardinality threshold.

Tetrahedral flags
-----------------
For each v introduce t_v equivalent to the OR of two sufficient census
families: rank exactly4 and either weight8/energy64 with one4-pair
direction, or weight11/energy205. A rank interval containing another rank
does not qualify. In either family, whenever p_v(d)=4 the quotient pair
set in F2^5/<d> is a nonplanar four-set. Round7's
support13/four_pair_cut/FOUR_PAIR_CAPACITY_LEMMA.txt supplies the elementary
proof and records the excluded planar exceptions.

Two nonplanar four-sets in the quotient four-space have sumset size at
most14. Their three-dimensional direction spaces meet in at least a
plane; after excluding one missing nonzero difference from each set, a
common nonzero difference remains. Its two endpoint pairs force two
distinct collisions among the16 ordered sums. This is a bound on each
individual edge, irrespective of how many edges have the same residue
direction. The ring carry translates that edge sumset and does not change
its cardinality.

A second, explicit capacity upper bound
--------------------------------------
For every edge e={a,b} with direction d=a+b, create16 fresh unary bits
c[e,j], j=1..16. They mean that a new capacity variable C_e is at least j.
Monotonicity gives a true prefix of length C_e. For each pair-count choice
p,q, the inherited sumset table supplies C_e<=M[p,q]. If M[p,q]<16 this is
encoded by

    not c[e,M[p,q]+1] OR not y[a,d,p] OR not y[b,d,q].

Also impose

    not c[e,15] OR not t_a OR not t_b
        OR not y[a,d,4] OR not y[b,d,4].

The code uses zero-based bits[cap], so the displayed threshold M+1 is
exactly bits[M], and the tetrahedral threshold15 is bits[14]. For each
nonzero d, the bidirectional totalizer requires the sum of the new edge
capacities in that direction to be at least16.

To extend the assignment of an actual Kakeya configuration, set C_e to
the actual cardinality of that edge's carried quotient sumset. All table
bounds hold, and whenever the tetrahedral antecedents hold the new14
bound holds. The union of the actual edge sumsets covers all16 lifts of
d, so their cardinalities sum to at least16. Hence every added direction
constraint is satisfied. Overlaps between edges are allowed; using their
sum is an upper bound, which is exactly the necessary condition required.

The new capacities are separate from the original baseline capacities.
Their witnesses may both be chosen from the same actual geometry, so no
identification with hidden baseline variables is necessary. Every original
variable and clause is left untouched. Disabling the extension must give
byte-identical DIMACS output; the finite controls check this for all four
target supports.

Consequently every actual counterexample satisfying the declared scope
extends to a satisfying assignment of the strengthened CNF. A refutation
checked against that exact new CNF excludes that scope, conditional on the
inherited premises and these geometric lemmas. An old-baseline refutation
is not claimed merely because a strengthened formula is refuted. RUP-unit
search preprocessing remains untrusted: its complete prefixed proof is
checked against the exact unaugmented strengthened CNF.

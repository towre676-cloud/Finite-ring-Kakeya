Independent review of the three-direction price bound
=====================================================

Reviewer: /root/support13_certification
Scope: the frozen Round7 middle-profile frontier, using a common necessary
condition for Z/4Z and F2[epsilon]/(epsilon^2). No SAT search or inherited
fibre-spectrum census is used in this review.

Mathematical argument
---------------------
Assume exactly nine residues carry fibres of weight at least four. Let
H be those residues. The analytic nine-point difference bound gives at
least three directions outside D(H). No three such directions can form
the nonzero part of a binary plane: otherwise H has at most one point
in each of the plane's eight cosets. Thus any three distinct missing
directions d1,d2,d3 are linearly independent.

Consider only these three directions. Each must cover sixteen lifted
directions, so the total required edge capacity is at least 48. No edge
in them joins two heavy residues. A contributing heavy-light edge has
capacity at most the heavy fibre's pair count, because the light pair
set is a singleton. A contributing light-light edge has capacity at
most one. Carries only translate these quotient sumsets.

For a heavy fibre of weight w, let k be the number of these directions
in which it has a contributing heavy-light edge. Then 0 <= k <= 3, and
the sum of its contributing pair counts is at most E_k(w), the maximum
number of induced edges of a w-point subset along k independent binary
directions. Let u count contributing light-light edges. Consequently

    48 <= sum_heavy E_{k_v}(w_v) + u.

Let n2 and n3 be the numbers of light fibres of weights two and three.
A weight-two fibre supplies at most one incidence in these directions;
a weight-three fibre supplies at most two because its three positive
directions form a plane, whereas d1,d2,d3 are independent. Therefore

    sum_heavy k_v + 2u <= A := n2 + 2n3.

For any integer lambda >= 1, set

    C_lambda(w) = max_{0 <= k <= 3}(E_k(w) - lambda*k).

Every heavy term is at most lambda*k_v + C_lambda(w_v), and u <= 2lambda*u.
Combining the preceding bounds proves the necessary condition

    48 <= lambda*A + sum_heavy C_lambda(w_v).

A strictly smaller right-hand side excludes the complete weight profile
for every placement and fibre shape. This argument does not depend on
the SAT exclusions of marked support roles.

Independent finite computation
------------------------------
For k independent directions, the ambient graph is the disjoint union
of 2^(5-k) copies of the k-dimensional cube. I enumerated every subset
of each cube, maximizing the number of induced edges at each weight.
The local tables are

    g0 = (0,0)
    g1 = (0,0,1)
    g2 = (0,0,1,2,4)
    g3 = (0,0,1,2,4,5,7,9,12).

There are 278 enumerated subsets including the two trivial k=0 cases,
or 276 nontrivial cases for k=1,2,3. Exact max-plus convolution over the
cosets gives E_k(w) for all weights zero through 32. This is independent
of the archived complete fibre-spectrum and sumset censuses.

For every one of the 2,187 nine-heavy profiles in the original frontier,
I also solved the finite allocation relaxation independently: choose
k_v in {0,1,2,3}, pay k_v incidences for value E_{k_v}(w_v), and allow
remaining incidences to buy light-light contributions at cost two each.
Its optimum equals the minimum of the displayed price bound over
lambda=1,...,9 for every one of those profiles. Equality with this DP
is corroboration; soundness of any individual strict dual certificate
already follows from the preceding inequalities.

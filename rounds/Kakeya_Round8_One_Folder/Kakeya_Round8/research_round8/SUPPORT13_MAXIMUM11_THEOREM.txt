Round8 branch theorem: maximum eleven, support thirteen
======================================================

Statement
---------
For R=Z/4Z and R=F2[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), no Kakeya subset of R^5 with
at most93 points has exact maximum fibre size11 and pair-active residue
support size13, conditional on the inherited reductions and the reviewed
necessary-condition encoder.

This is a complete computer-assisted exclusion of that branch.  It
implies that any remaining maximum-eleven counterexample must have
pair-active support14,15 or16.  The unrestricted94-point lower bound,
the middle maxima12..18, and the dimension-four target remain open.

Reduction to the four marked formulas
------------------------------------
The unchanged Round7 ledger already reduces the support-thirteen,
maximum-eleven branch to affine support classes109,110,111.  All largest-
fibre positions in those classes are accounted for by the explicit maps
in research_round7/geometry/SUPPORT13_MARKING_MAP.json.  Combining those
maps with the preceding certificates leaves exactly these four roles:

    class109: 377850134
    class111: 411136406
    class110: 671697334 and2185856310.

The integers are masks of the nonzero residues after placing a largest
fibre at residue zero.  Equal largest fibres are allowed.  No assumption
that the largest fibre occupies an arbitrary symmetry-equivalent role
is made: every necessary role is either inherited as closed or checked
here.  merge_round8.py verifies this coverage and closes the classes
only after all their marked roles are covered.

Necessary conditions encoded
----------------------------
The frozen encoder assigns one complete census spectrum to each fibre,
one directional pair count to every incidence, a consistent affine
direction space and maximal-pair subspace, and total weight at most93.
Every nonzero residue direction must receive capacity at least16 from
its actual support edges.  The usual capacity table bounds each edge.

Round8 appends two new geometric restrictions.  First, thirteen fibres
of maximum size at most eleven require at least ten fibres of size at
least four, and those ten-or-more heavy residues must realize all31
nonzero differences.  This theorem needs no total-weight budget or
fibre census.  Second, two tetrahedral four-point quotient pair-sets
have sumset size at most14.  The new formula applies this bound to
every applicable edge, including directions with several edges.

Each new edge capacity has explicitly allocated unary variables.  It
does not depend on inferred numbering of hidden old variables.  For
an actual Kakeya set, assign both old and new capacity families their
actual edge sumset sizes.  Each bound holds, while their sum in every
direction is at least16.  Thus every actual counterexample extends
to a satisfying assignment of its corresponding strengthened formula.
The ring carry only translates each edge sumset; these cardinality
bounds are necessary in both rings.

Exact refutations
-----------------
All four formulas were frozen before search.  Each produced a complete
DRAT refutation within its120-second search budget.  Proof reconstruction
and checking were additional work.  The complete proof, including the
untrusted search preprocessor's RUP prefix, was checked against the
original strengthened formula.  DRAT-trim reconstructed LRAT, and the
pinned CakeML checker accepted that LRAT against the same CNF hash.

Every proof was then trimmed, and the compact replacement was freshly
checked against that unchanged formula.  The portable replayer rebuilds
the exact formula byte for byte and checks all four compact proofs.
The large original DRAT streams are unnecessary for portable replay;
their hashes and full production provenance remain recorded.

The accepted searches used the ten-heavy/full-cover theorem and the
all-edge tetrahedral restriction.  The later eleven-heavy theorem,
general missing-direction incidence bound, hyperplane cuts and Q211
paired-space cuts are separate results.  They are not retroactively
credited as premises or speed improvements in these four searches.

Trust boundary and accounting
-----------------------------
The theorem uses the inherited support reduction, complete fibre and
capacity census data, mathematical soundness lemmas, and the Python
encoder faithfully implementing those lemmas.  Exact proof checking
removes trust in the SAT solver's answer.  It does not formalize the
whole mathematical reduction or Python encoder in a proof assistant.

The combined direct exact-eleven coverage is now39 of55 marked cases,
including the four new refutations, in the full support-thirteen
catalogue.  Full closure of this maximum uses
the inherited affine-class exclusions for the other possibilities.
The maximum-eleven affine ledger changes45 to42.  The four marked SAT
cases remove no whole middle-maximum weight profiles.  Separately, the
new incidence and price theorems exclude950 complete middle profiles,
reducing183476 to182526. Intermediate cumulative counts16,128,175,745
and the final950 are separately recorded without duplicate credit.
This second deduction holds for every placement
of those profiles.  The overall pair-active-support minimum remains13.

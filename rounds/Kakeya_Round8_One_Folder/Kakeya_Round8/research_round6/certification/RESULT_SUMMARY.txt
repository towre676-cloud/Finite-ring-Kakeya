Maximum-19 certification: completed result
=========================================

All 38 exact formulas produced by the supplied encode_cnf2.py have been
freshly refuted and accepted through the pinned DRAT-trim to LRAT to
CakeML route. The case universe is exactly 13 marked support-11 classes
and 25 marked support-12 classes. Tied maxima are allowed; no outer-weight-14
cap is used. The common necessary-condition interpretation covers both
rings, subject to the separate mathematical and encoder soundness review.

A second, complete replay forced the actual compressed .xz payloads. It
regenerated every original CNF byte-identically, checked the full marked
support contract and semantic-source hashes, verified all original CNF,
DRAT, and LRAT byte hashes, and reran the mandatory CakeML checker on all
38 formulas. Every case passed. This replay took 200.917345 seconds.

The uploaded archive did not supply the historical CNFs, DRATs, or earlier
encoder revision. None of those old proofs is claimed to have been checked.
All newly generated CNF hashes differ from the historical log. The present
acceptance result concerns the exact supplied encoder, so it does not assume
that the earlier and current revisions are equivalent.

The original production route used CaDiCaL 2.1.2 as an untrusted search tool.
Summed per-case timings were 387.805322 solver seconds, 274.515111 DRAT-trim
seconds, and 183.916298 CakeML seconds. The jobs ran with at most two solver
workers. Per-case receipts retain full hashes, transcripts, and timings.

All 114 official blobs were losslessly compressed and round-trip checked:
  Original CNFs: 260,322,324 bytes; .xz copies: 18,457,456 bytes.
  Original DRATs: 1,312,148,309 bytes; .xz copies: 138,365,336 bytes.
  Accepted LRATs: 3,592,052,780 bytes; .xz copies: 297,258,580 bytes.
  All payloads: 5,164,523,413 bytes; .xz copies: 454,081,372 bytes.

A replay distribution containing exact CNFs and accepted LRATs needs
315,716,036 compressed payload bytes. The original DRAT search traces are
redundant for that replay, but were preserved here with their exact hashes.
The verifier has an explicit --lrat-only mode for a distribution that omits
those traces; its receipt does not claim to have checked absent DRAT bytes.

One integrity incident was caught by the final hash gate: a previously
accepted DRAT file had become truncated. Its original CNF and checked LRAT
were intact. Regeneration restored precisely the original DRAT byte length
and SHA256, and both checkers accepted again. No hash mismatch was waived.
The complete compressed replay above passed after this repair. The cause
is undetermined; see integrity_incident/INTEGRITY_INCIDENT.json.

Scope limits:
  * This verifies exact formula unsatisfiability and binds every formula
    to its declared input through deterministic regeneration.
  * It is not a formal proof of the encoder's mathematical soundness.
  * It does not by itself exclude maximum 19 on larger support sizes.
    The complete maximum-19 stratum still uses the inherited reduction
    to support 11 or 12.
  * Neither the unrestricted dimension-five lower bound of 94 nor the
    general all-dimension formula is claimed here.

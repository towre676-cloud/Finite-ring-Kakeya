Exact direction models for two dense fibre types
===============================================

Status: constructive encoding design, derived from the proved complement
lemmas. No new encoder, benchmark, SAT certificate or exclusion is claimed.
Source: DENSE_FIBRE_COMPLEMENT_LEMMAS.md and its complete finite replay.

These models can replace incomplete collections of local forbidding
clauses for the two four-dimensional types with trivial maximal-pair
subspace. They describe the entire labelled pair profile of either type.
Only incident directions need to be linked to the existing SAT variables.
They do not prescribe the fibre's translation or settle global coverage.

1. Weight twelve, energy 294
--------------------------

Let H be the selected four-dimensional linear direction space. Choose a
three-space J contained in H and a nonzero vector u in J. For every nonzero
direction d, impose the following conditional profile when this type is
selected:

  p(d)=5  exactly when d is in J and d is not u;
  p(d)=4  exactly when d is in H and (d is outside J or d=u);
  p(d)=0  exactly when d is outside H.

Necessity is the proved four-point-complement classification. Conversely,
choose an ordered basis e1,e2,e3 of J whose sum is u. Such a basis exists:
an invertible map of J can send the sum of a fixed basis to any prescribed
nonzero vector. Put B={0,e1,e2,e3} and A=H minus B. Its six pair differences
are precisely J minus {0,u}, so A realizes the displayed profile.

Thus the model is exact for this fibre type. In a fixed H there are
15 choices of J and 7 choices of u, giving 105 profiles. Across all 31
four-spaces in F2^5, there are 3255 labelled profiles. A structured encoding
can reuse the existing H selector, choose J, and select u; it need not
introduce one selector for each of all 3255 profiles.

2. Weight eleven, energy 205
--------------------------

Choose five distinct nonzero vectors c0,...,c4 in the selected four-space H
whose XOR is zero. These conditions already make them a five-circuit.
Indeed, a proper zero-sum subset cannot have size 1 or 2; a subset of size 3
or 4 would leave a zero-sum complement of size 2 or 1, also impossible.
Every four are therefore independent, so the five vectors span H.

Put C={c0,...,c4}. Conditional on selecting this type, for nonzero d require

  p(d)=3  exactly when d is in C;
  p(d)=4  exactly when d is in H but not in C;
  p(d)=0  exactly when d is outside H.

Necessity follows from the five-point-complement lemma. For sufficiency,
set fi=c0+ci for i=1,...,4. The four fi are independent: a relation among
them would induce a relation among the five ci, and their unique nonzero
relation uses all five; the coefficient on c0 would then be four rather
than one modulo 2, a contradiction. Their sum is c0. Consequently the
complement of B={0,f1,f2,f3,f4} in H has its five three-pair directions
exactly at c0 and c0+fi=ci. This realizes the required profile.

There are 168 profiles in a fixed H, hence 5208 in F2^5. A direct binary
encoding needs five 5-bit vectors, their nonzero/distinctness constraints,
membership in H, and one five-bit XOR equation. Requiring their integer
labels to increase removes the 5! ordering symmetry without discarding
any profile. The last vector can be represented as the XOR of the first
four; all five must still satisfy the order and nonzero conditions.

3. How to use this next
----------------------

Gate each model by the existing selector for its weight, affine rank and
histogram, including all hidden-vector membership, nonzero, distinctness
and ordering constraints. Link the resulting counts to every existing incident-direction
pair variable. Keep the selected H consistent with the original encoder.
The constructions above give a direct one-way extension proof for every
real fibre of either type, as well as local sufficiency for its profile.

These models address fibres on which the earlier K-coset equalities are
vacuous because K={0}. Benchmark them against the frozen baseline on the
same hard support 13 cases, with identical time limits and complete proof
checking. Their mathematical exactness does not imply faster SAT search.

Redundant search units, with a proof against the unchanged formula
================================================================

The fixed-maximum joint encoder still represents the hub's known weight
through spectrum selectors. In a fixed maximum18 case, each of the first
18 hub weight bits is logically true. Its defining clauses say that it is
the OR of all eligible hub spectrum selectors. That truth need not be
exposed immediately by unit propagation before a spectrum is selected.
The same issue affects the first two weight bits of every outer fibre.

A simple resolution rule exposes such constants. Suppose the original
formula contains a positive clause z1 OR ... OR zk, and every zi implies
u through a binary clause (-zi OR u). Then u is entailed. Adding u is a
reverse-unit-propagation step: assuming -u forces all zi false and
contradicts their original positive clause.

rup_unit_solver.py finds common binary consequences of positive clauses,
including identity implications. It gives the original formula plus the
derived units to an untrusted CaDiCaL search. It then concatenates the
exact unit clauses as a DRAT prefix with that search's ordinary textual
DRAT output. The production and replay checkers validate this concatenated
proof against the ORIGINAL exact CNF, whose bytes remain unchanged.

Consequently the preprocessing algorithm is not a new mathematical trust
assumption. A faulty transformation cannot silently establish a theorem:
the final certificate must still refute the original necessary-condition
formula. The optional wrapper is a search aid, not a strengthened encoder.
Portable proof replay does not execute the wrapper or any SAT solver.

First result
------------

For marked support Tmask10069398 and exact maximum18, the wrapper exposed
47 redundant search units. The solve completed in 23.834041 seconds.
DRAT-trim reconstructed LRAT and the pinned CakeML checker accepted it
against the unchanged original CNF. The complete solve/check/compression
pipeline took 59.131687 seconds. This excludes that support/maximum case
in both rings under the documented common necessary-condition encoding.

The earlier unaugmented search on the same exact CNF timed out at twenty
seconds. These unequal limits do not establish a search-speed improvement.
The certificate is useful regardless of whether a longer unaugmented run
would also have succeeded.

The delivered case contains input.cnf.xz, proof.drat.xz, the exact prefix
rup_unit_prefix.txt, and rup_unit_preprocessing.txt, which is JSON with all
units and their original-clause witnesses. The collection manifest binds
the prefix and metadata hashes to the original CNF and complete proof.

Validation and process supervision
----------------------------------

The finite unit-discovery check exhausts all sixteen assignments on each
of one thousand reproducibly generated four-variable formulas. Every
derived unit holds on every original satisfying assignment. Separate
checker controls accept a correctly prefixed refutation, reject an
augmented-only empty proof against the original formula, and reject a
wrong-unit refutation of a satisfiable formula. These controls themselves
claim no Kakeya exclusion.

The current producer starts a separate process group and becomes a Linux
child subreaper. Time and size limits stop the complete group, including
the wrapper's CaDiCaL child. Every live DRAT stream is counted, including
search_solver.drat before concatenation. Publication waits until the
direct process and adopted descendants are joined and the process group
is absent. Controls explicitly exercise a growing grandchild trace, a
parent that exits leaving a writer, and a wall timeout; no writer remains.

The original producer revision is retained in producer_snapshots solely
as historical provenance. Use the current certify_support13.py for new
searches. Concurrent disjoint batches must choose distinct --worker and
--tag values so their checker build outputs and progress receipts do not
collide. Completed case files remain immutable.

Example (provided solver path exists):

    python3 research_round7/support13/certify_support13.py --lower 18 --upper 18 --mask 360808918 --limit 60 --tag rup_trial60 --worker rup_trial --solver /absolute/path/to/research_round7/support13/rup_unit_solver.py

One-way soundness of the unique-edge four-pair extension
======================================================

Assume a Kakeya set in either ring with total at most93 and the declared
marked support and maximum interval. The frozen Round6 soundness argument
supplies an assignment to its baseline spectrum and directional-count
variables. Each actual fibre selects its actual census spectrum.

The extension flags only two spectrum families whose entire recorded rank
interval is exactly4: weight8/energy64 with one four-pair direction, and
weight11/energy205. By FOUR_PAIR_CAPACITY_LEMMA.txt, whenever a flagged
fibre has four pairs in a direction, its quotient pair-set is a nonplanar
four-set. Assign every new type-disjunction variable the OR of its selected
eligible spectrum literals. Its defining clauses then hold automatically.

For a direction with one residue edge, the actual Kakeya set must cover
all16 lifted directions through that edge. If both endpoint type flags
and both four-pair literals were true, the lemma bounds the actual edge's
quotient sumset by14. A ring carry merely translates that sumset, so it
cannot increase its cardinality. This contradicts Kakeya coverage. Thus
the new four-literal forbidding clause holds on every real instance.

No assertion is made for directions with two or more residue edges. No
assertion is made for the planar twelve-point exception, unflagged types,
or endpoint counts other than exactly4. Rank intervals that include5 are
not flagged. The code appends clauses and auxiliary OR definitions while
leaving every original variable and clause unchanged. Disabling the cut
is checked to regenerate identical DIMACS bytes.

Consequently every real Kakeya counterexample extends to a satisfying
assignment of the new formula. A checked UNSAT proof for that exact new
formula therefore gives an exclusion, conditional on the existing census
and baseline obligations plus the elementary lemma above. This complete
one-way argument is a mathematical/code audit, not a Lean formalization.

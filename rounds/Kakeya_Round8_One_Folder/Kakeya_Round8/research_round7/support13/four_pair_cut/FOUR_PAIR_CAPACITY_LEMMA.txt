Four-pair edges: a sharp capacity14 obstruction
=============================================

Status: analytic lemma and finite verification. Implementation controls
and proof-producing trial outcomes are tracked separately. This lemma
alone is not counted as a SAT exclusion or a search-speed result.

1. Two nonplanar four-sets cannot tile the four-space
--------------------------------------------------

Let P,Q be four-element subsets of F2^4. If neither is an affine plane,
then

    |P+Q| <= 14.

Translate a nonplanar four-set to {0,e1,e2,e3}, with the three ei linearly
independent. Its nonzero difference set is H minus {0,u}, where H is the
three-dimensional direction space and u=e1+e2+e3. Two three-spaces in
F2^4 meet in dimension at least two, hence in at least three nonzero
vectors. Removing the two possible missing vectors leaves a common
nonzero difference delta.

Choose P-pair {p,p+delta} and Q-pair {q,q+delta}. In the sixteen ordered
sum representations, (p,q) and (p+delta,q+delta) give the same sum;
(p,q+delta) and (p+delta,q) give another common sum. These four ordered
pairs are distinct, and the two resulting sums differ by nonzero delta.
Thus at least two of the sixteen possible distinct values are lost.

The bound is sharp. P={0,1,2,4} and Q={0,7,8,14}, in binary coordinates,
give fourteen sums. Consequently a four-pair by four-pair edge can have
capacity16 only if at least one endpoint's quotient pair-set is an
affine plane. Two tetrahedral pair-sets have capacity at most14.

2. The period-free eleven-point type always supplies tetrahedra
-------------------------------------------------------------

Let A be an eleven-point subset of an affine four-flat F=t+H in F2^5,
and suppose its energy Q(A)=sum_{d!=0} p_A(d)^2 is205. Write B=F minus A.
For nonzero d in H the complement identity gives

    p_A(d)=3+p_B(d).

Since |B|=5, its pair energy is10+6 Z4(B), and sum p_B(d)=10. Therefore

    Q(A)=15*9+6*10+10+6 Z4(B)=205+6 Z4(B).

So B contains no affine plane. Five points in an affine three-space
necessarily have a nonzero even affine relation of weight4. Hence B has
affine rank4 and is an affine-independent five-set.

If p_A(d)=4, then p_B(d)=1. Projection to H/<d> identifies exactly one
pair of B and leaves four distinct, affinely independent projected
points. A four-point affine relation after projection would lift either
to a four-point relation in B or, after toggling the identified pair, to
another four-point relation in B; both are impossible.

The quotient pair-set P_A(d) consists exactly of the d-cosets containing
no B-point. It is the complement of those four projected B-points in the
eight-point quotient three-space. The complement of an affine plane
there is its parallel affine plane. Therefore the complement of a
nonplanar four-set is also nonplanar. Thus every p_A(d)=4 direction of
this eleven-point type supplies a tetrahedral quotient pair-set.

This argument concerns affine rank4 explicitly. The existing census
records the weight11/energy205 type with affine rank4; an implementation
must retain that rank hypothesis if using another spectrum catalogue.

3. An eight-point noncube with four pairs supplies a tetrahedron
--------------------------------------------------------------

If |A|=8 and p_A(d)=4, A is invariant under translation by d. Its image
P_A(d) has four points. The affine rank of A is one plus the affine rank
of P_A(d). Thus an eight-point noncube with four d-pairs has affine rank4
and a nonplanar four-point quotient. Its full pair spectrum is

    one direction with4 pairs, twelve directions with2 pairs,
    eighteen directions with0 pairs,

so its energy is64. A cube instead has seven maximal directions and
planar quotient pair-sets. The64-energy type is exactly the relevant
eight-point type for this cut.

4. A refined twelve-point type
-----------------------------

For A of weight12 and energy294 in an affine four-flat, B=F minus A is
a nonplanar four-set. Let J be its three-dimensional direction space,
and let u be the unique missing nonzero direction in B+B. In each of the
eight directions d in H minus J, p_A(d)=4 and the quotient pair-set is
nonplanar. At the exceptional direction d=u, p_A(d)=4 but the quotient
pair-set is an affine plane. The other six nonzero J-directions have
five pairs. The existing exact dense-type model already identifies J,u,
so this distinction can be used without enumerating full fibre shapes.

5. Safe clause schemas
----------------------

For an actual residue edge {a,b} of direction d, if both endpoint quotient
pair-sets are four-point tetrahedra, replace the generic cap16 by cap14.
Carries only translate this edge's quotient sumset, so the cardinality
bound is common to Z/4Z and the dual-number ring.

In particular, select either the weight8/energy64 noncube type or the
weight11/energy205 rank4 type at each endpoint. If both directional pair
counts equal4, their edge capacity is at most14. On a direction having
only one residue edge, that combination is impossible, because the
single edge must supply all16 lifted directions. This produces the
conditional clause

    not(Type_a and Type_b and p_a(d)=4 and p_b(d)=4).

With one Boolean for each endpoint's disjunction of the two types, only
one four-literal clause per unique residue edge is needed. For a general
edge whose unary capacity bits are available, add the corresponding
conditional bound capacity<=14. The exceptional twelve-point flag can
be included only with its correct d-outside-J condition; its planar
exception d=u must remain allowed.

These clauses are new geometric information for the spectrum relaxation,
not mere logical preprocessing of its old CNF. They require the one-way
mathematical lemma above, unlike the separate proof-prefixed RUP units.
Any eventual UNSAT certificate must be checked against its exact new CNF.

THE NEXT ATTACK AFTER ROUND7
===========================

Decision
--------
The best direction is to combine exact edge geometry with systematic
heavy/light prices. This route has already produced a theorem in dimension3
and 3,006 universal profile exclusions in dimension 5. Plain longer runs of
the same loose relaxation are no longer the main mathematical plan.

----------------------------------------

The immediate strengthening is the proven cardinality constraint that at

requires an explicit edge-capacity interface; attaching the cut to guessed

These modifications have mathematical justification but no credited new
UNSAT result in this checkpoint. A future completed refutation must bind
to the exact strengthened formula and pass the mandatory checker.

2. Strengthen the dual where it is still lossy
---------------------------------------------
For a selected heavy residue setH, remove its internal direction setD(H).
Every remaining edge touches a light fibre. With light ceilingell,
t=floor(ell/2), and integer priceu>=t, define

    g(p)=max(0, max_{1<=q<=t}(2B(p,q)-u*q)).

For the present certificates t<=3, and the elementary capacities are

    B(p,1)=p,
    B(p,2)=min(2p,16),
    B(p,3)=min(3p,p+8,16).

IfG_w is the largest sum_d g(p_A(d)) among actual weightw spectra, then
every configuration satisfies

    32*(31-|D(H)|)
       <=u*sum_light C(w,2)+sum_heavy G_w.

The 3,006 accepted certificates use the coarser universal bound
|D(H)|<=C(|H|,2). Optional promotion of a nominally light fibre toH improves
six certificates; the exact heavy positions are stored, so replay does
not trust the optimization method. All arithmetic is integer.

The next substantial improvement should use the ACTUAL D(H) for each
support placement and the actual directions left outsideD(H). The current
right side pays for all heavy directions, including those never charged.
Removing that waste while enforcing one consistent direction space and
period structure should strengthen the bound much more than changing a
single scalar coefficient. Weight-dependent or direction-dependent prices
can be found by an LP, then cleared to integers and checked exactly.

The sharp symbolic bounds |D(H)|<=25 for eight heavy residues and<=28 for
nine are ready. They produced no additional profiles in the current scalar
screen; retain that negative result. Their main value may emerge when
coupled to actual support geometry and restricted directional resources.

3. Use exact models selectively
-------------------------------
Two exact-shape/point encodings now exist. The common-ring exact-shape model
uses real pair sets and actual edge sumset cardinalities but relaxes overlaps
between different edges. Independent fibre translations preserve those
cardinalities, so that relaxation may normalize each fibre separately.

The ring-specific exact-point model requires actual coverage of every lift.
It must use the correct carry and one COMMON affine tangent shear. The
arbitrary-dimensional preservation of line coverage under that common shear
has been compiled and axiom-audited in Lean. Do not import the independent
translation normalization from the cardinality relaxation into this model.

The exact-point route completed dimension 2 and 3, and 14 of 56 dimension 4 cases.
It did not settle the harder dimension 5 trial. The right use is to verify
small complete slices or geometrically narrowed instances, while the dual
and compatibility lemmas remove broad families beforehand.

4. Convert the new mathematics into a smaller trusted core
----------------------------------------------------------
The complete elementary dimension 3 proof is now a practical formalization
target. The next Lean bridge for dimension 5 is the geometric construction
of the injective endpoint-direction charges and the count of directions
outsideD(H). The abstract summation and strict-gap contradiction already
compile in ChargingDual.lean. Spectrum completeness and Python-to-CNF
faithfulness remain separate obligations.

Success criteria
----------------
A whole support slice is closed only when every required marked role and
maximum interval is covered. A whole weight profile is removed only by a
universal certificate or complete coverage of every placement. A timeout
does not reduce either count. The exact 22-point theorem is complete;
46 in dimension 4,94 in dimension 5, and the general formula remain open.
